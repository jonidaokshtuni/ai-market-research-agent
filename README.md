# AI Market Agent

An AI-powered market intelligence platform designed to help businesses understand their markets, monitor competitors, identify strategic changes, and make data-driven decisions.

## Overview

AI Market Agent combines autonomous AI research agents, web intelligence, and automated competitor monitoring to transform market information into actionable business insights.

## Key Features

- **AI-Powered Market Research:** Conduct automated research and generate structured market insights.
- **Competitor Intelligence:** Track competitors, analyze their positioning, products, strengths, and weaknesses.
- **Automated Competitor Monitoring:** Schedule daily or weekly competitor research in the background.
- **AI Change Detection:** Identify meaningful competitor changes and strategic developments between research snapshots.
- **Research History:** Store and revisit previous competitor analyses and research snapshots.
- **Analytics Dashboard:** Explore market intelligence and research insights through interactive visualizations.
- **Report Generation:** Generate structured research reports and export them for further use.
